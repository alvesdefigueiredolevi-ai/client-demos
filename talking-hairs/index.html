SENSORY-CONSCIOUS CARE

An appointment built around your comfort.

Different clients need different environments. Demi welcomes conversations about sensory preferences, communication, breaks and personal boundaries before the appointment begins.

Clear, direct communication

Transparent explanations of every step, product, and technique before it touches your hair.

No pressure to make conversation

Silent appointments are fully welcomed. Read, relax, or listen to your headphones in peace.

Sensory preferences welcomed

Preferences regarding lighting, cape tightness, music volume, and scents are respected.

Breaks when needed

Need to stretch, stand up, or take a breath? Pauses are readily accommodated.

Respect for personal boundaries

Your comfort, bodily autonomy, and identity choices guide the entire process.

Alternative communication welcomed

Feel free to bring reference photos, notes, or use text-based communication apps.

Personalized consultation

Thorough, patient pre-service discussions so you feel confident and in control.

Judgment-free environment

A private suite where all hair histories, identities, and sensory needs are honored.

Accommodations can be discussed with Demi before booking. Reach out anytime with specific accommodation questions.

BEHIND THE CHAIR

Meet Demi Rivera

Demi is an AuDHD hairstylist and creative professional with 14 years of experience. She specializes in serving disability and LGBTQ+ communities while cutting and coloring all hair types and styles.

Her particular favorites include mullets, shags and expressive color. Every appointment begins with listening and ends with hair that feels more like the person wearing it.
