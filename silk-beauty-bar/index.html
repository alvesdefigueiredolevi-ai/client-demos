Meet the Artist

Beauty That Feels Like You

Welcome to Silk Beauty Bar, a private luxury sanctuary located within Salons by JC in Cherry Creek. Owned and operated by Alma C. Garcia, the studio was created with a clear vision: to provide thoughtful, tailored hair artistry in an inviting and relaxed atmosphere.

Alma offers personalized hair and beauty services designed to enhance each client’s natural features and personal style. From nuanced dimensional balayage to precision haircuts and bridal hair and makeup, every appointment is dedicated entirely to your vision.
