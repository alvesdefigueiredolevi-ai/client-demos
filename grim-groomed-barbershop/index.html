Grooming Built Around the Individual

Grim & Groomed Barbershop is an independent barbershop located in Centennial, Colorado, led by barber Miranda Beers. Every service is focused on personal attention, clean execution, and an unhurried, comfortable grooming experience tailored specifically to you.

Operating from a private studio setting within My Salon Suite, Miranda delivers direct, one-on-one barbering with consistent precision from start to finish.
