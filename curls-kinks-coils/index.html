Meet Your Texture Specialist

Care That Starts With Understanding Your Hair.

At Curls Kinks & Coils, Rose Curly specializes in cutting, styling, and caring for different natural textures, from loose, fluid waves to tight, intricate coils. Every appointment is an individualized experience centered around what your unique pattern needs.

Rather than treating textured hair as a one-size-fits-all formula, Rose approaches every client with careful listening, precise technique, and comprehensive hair education. You leave the studio not only with defined, radiant hair, but also with clear at-home routines tailored to your lifestyle.

Honoring Every Pattern

Every Pattern Has Its Own Story.

Service recommendations are personalized according to texture, density, condition, goals, and daily routine.

Waves

Soft S-patterns that thrive on lightweight hydration, volume-enhancing cuts, and gentle frizz control that won't weigh down your natural flow.

Curls

Springy spirals and loops that benefit from intentional shaping, customized moisture layering, and clumping techniques for long-lasting definition.

Kinks

Tightly wound Z-patterns requiring deep hydration sealing, delicate detangling methods, and specialized cuts that honor natural shrinkage and volume.

Coils

Dense, compact coils that flourish with intensive conditioning, low-manipulation protective styling, and scalp-to-tip nourishment.
